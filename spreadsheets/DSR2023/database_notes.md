# DSR 2023: database notes

Read this before you choose items from this database. The general part says what the database is and how its items are built. Each `## Category:` section describes one chapter (sub-head): what it covers, how its items are arranged and how to pick the right one. Read the section for every category you take items from.

## About

- **Book:** CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates 2023 (civil), Vol. 1 and 2, and Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023. Published by the Director General, CPWD, Nirman Bhawan, New Delhi. Foreword dated 12 July 2023. Effective from 16-10-2023, including corrections up to 10-11-2023.
- **Supersedes:** DSR 2021. Items not in use were deleted and new items were added. Gaps in the numbering are deleted items.
- **Rates basis:** Delhi market rates of materials in April 2023. Labour at the minimum wages from 01-04-2023. Cost index 107 with reference to the CPWD Delhi Plinth Area Rates 2021 (base 100 on 01-04-2021). Outside Delhi, apply the cost index of the place to the schedule rates.
- **Specifications:** CPWD Specifications 2019 Vol. I and II, with the correction slips up to date. The item text and the specification together define what an item includes.
- **Schedule rates are complete.** They include materials, labour, T&P and sundries, 1 % water charges, GST on works contract at 18 %, 15 % contractor's profit and overheads and 1 % labour cess.
- **Basic rates (resources)** exclude GST, contractor's profit and overheads. The analyses add them.
- **Guardrail:** do not add GST, cess, water charges or contractor's profit and overheads to schedule rates. Do not use a basic rate (resource) as a BOQ item: a resource enters an estimate only through a rate analysis. Do not use a MORTARS or SUB ANALYSIS item as a BOQ item: their rates are bare cost without overheads.
- **Source data:** the tables of an estimating program that holds DSR 2023 (schedule, analyses, basic rates). The codes, units and rates were checked against the printed DSR 2023 and the printed rate is used. The carriage rates (1.1, 1.2) and the resource categories and descriptions come from the CPWD master workbook of DSR 2023 and DAR 2023.

## How the items are built

- **Codes** follow the book: chapter.item[.sub-item[.sub-sub-item]], for example 5.1.2 or 9.27.2.1.1. A suffix letter (5.3A, 4.1.5B) is a separate item, usually the same work with other materials. Most "A" items use manufactured sand or Recycled Concrete Aggregate (RCA) in place of natural sand and aggregate.
- **Headings and items.** A row with no unit and rate 0 is a heading: its text is the common specification. Its children add the variant (mix, size, thickness, finish). Always use a priced child, never the heading. The full meaning is heading text + child text. Where the book has a further level (for example 9.134 → 9.134.1 → 9.134.1.1 → 9.134.1.1.1), the intermediate heading text is at the start of the child's description, on its own line.
- **Main items and "Extra for" / "Deduct for" items.** Many chapters price a base item for normal conditions (up to floor five level, plain surfaces, normal lift and lead) and give separate items for the extra cost. Add the extra item for the same quantity. Typical extras: work above floor five level for each four floors (4.14, 5.38, 6.5, 6.14, 7.16, 13.22), work in or under water or in foul positions (4.15, 4.16, 5.31, 5.32, 6.24, 6.25, 7.34, 7.35), circular work, additional lift (2.26). "Deduct for" items (2.4, 2.5, 9.13, 9.15, 9.52, 11.42, 11.50, 16.76, 22.13) have a negative rate.
- **What a concrete item excludes.** Concrete and RCC items exclude centering and shuttering (4.3, 5.9), reinforcement (5.22) and finishing (plaster or 5.23, 5.24) unless the item says otherwise. Price each separately.
- **Analysis structure** (DAR 2023): materials and labour (W), add 1 % water charges (X), add GST on works contract by the multiplying factor 0.2127 (Y), add 15 % CP & OH (Z), add 1 % cess, round to 0.05 ("Say"). Some analyses price a lot (10 sqm, 100 metre) and divide by it. Where an analysis uses another schedule item at its schedule rate (the book's "W-A"), that item is in the group "ITEMS AT SCHEDULE RATES (A), INCLUSIVE OF OVERHEADS" after the additions, because its rate already includes them. Carriage and loading items (1.1.18, 1.3, 1.4) have no water charges.
- **Carriage in analyses is nil.** The basic rates are rates at site. The carriage codes (2200–2357) are in the analyses with rate 0; their reference gives the book's carriage rate. Add carriage from chapter 1 only where the work needs it.
- **Resource codes** are the book's basic rate codes: 0001–0092 hire charges of plants and machinery, 0100–0169 labour, 0222 onward materials, 2200–2357 carriage (9977 and 9988 are lump sum carriage, priced at 2.27 like sundries), 9999 sundries (L.S., rate 2.27). Three codes the book does not list (3229A, 7407A, 8587) come from the source analyses. Resources in the category SUB ANALYSIS have the code of a schedule item and take its rate (mortars 3.x, schedule items used inside other analyses, the sub-analyses x.SAn).
- **Units:** cum, sqm, metre, each, kg, quintal, tonne, 100 metre, joint, set, job, litre, 1000 Nos. Some items are priced per a compound unit, for example "cum per metre depth" (4.15, 6.24), "per bag of 50 kg cement used" (4.12, 4.18, 13.21), "cm per metre" (13.28), "per letter per cm height" (13.71). The quantity is the product that the unit names.
- **Analysis differences.** In 21 items the attached analysis does not give the book rate, because the source analysis differs from DAR 2023 (for example 4.5.1A–4.7.1A, 13.114, 13.116, 14.78, 17.7A). The rate is the book rate and the item's remarks give the analysis amount. About 35 analyses differ by less than Rs 1 (rounding). Check the analysis of such an item before you change resource rates in an estimate.
- **Percentage items** that the book prints as a percentage of another item (2.24.1 and 2.24.2: 20 % extra for excavation in or under water; 5.9.14: 20 % extra for shuttering in circular work) are not in the database. Add them as a percentage of the base item.

# DSR 2025 Horticulture and Landscaping: database notes

Read this before you choose items from this database. The general part says what the database is and how its items are built. Each `## Category:` section describes one sub-head: what it covers, how its items are arranged and how to pick the right one. Read the section for every category you take items from.

## About

- **Book:** CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates 2025, Horticulture and Landscaping (DSR/DAR 2025 Horticulture and Landscaping), published by the Director General, CPWD, New Delhi.
- **Use with:** DSR 2023 (civil) for building and civil work around a garden, and DSR (E&M) 2025 for electrical work. The "Civil Items" category here is only the small set of civil items the horticulture book repeats.
- **Rates basis:** Delhi market rates. Outside Delhi, apply the cost index of the place to the schedule rates.
- **Schedule rates are complete.** They include materials, labour, T&P, carriage as stated in the item, and contractor's profit and overheads. Do not add profit, overheads or GST on top.
- **Plants:** every plant item is "providing and stacking" or "providing, stacking and displaying": supply of the plant in a pot or bag at site. Planting, digging holes, soil preparation, maintenance and guards are separate items in Horticulture and Landscaping (chapter 2).
- **No analyses and no resources.** The database has schedule items only. Item rates are the published rates. For the rate build-up, see the printed DAR 2025 Horticulture.
- **Check before signing:** check an item against the printed book before you rely on its rate in a signed estimate.

## How the items are built

- **Codes** follow the book: chapter.item[.sub-item], for example 2.14.3 or 7.25. Chapters 2 to 12 are the sub-heads in the order of the categories below.
- **Civil Items codes have the prefix "C".** The book numbers them with DSR 2023 civil codes (2.1, 5.9, 13.61) that would clash with chapters 2, 5, 9, 10 and 13 of this book. C2.1.1 here is DSR 2023 item 2.1.1 as printed in the horticulture book. These items can differ in rate from DSR 2023, use the rate of this database for horticulture work.
- **Headings and items.** A row with no unit and rate 0 is a heading: its text is the common specification and its children add the variant (size, soil, finish). Always use a priced child, never the heading.
- **Units** are as printed: No. for plants, sqm and cum for earth work and turf, tray for seedlings, tree for transplantation and tree treatment, "/month" units (sqm/month, m/month, plant/month, pot/month, month) for maintenance priced for each month. For a maintenance item, quantity = area (or number) x months.
- **Plant sizes and pots** are in the item text (height, number of branches or leaves, pot or bag size). Choose the item whose plant, height and container match the specification. Plants are listed in alphabetical order by botanical name inside each category.

## Category: HORTICULTURE AND LANDSCAPING

Chapter 2, items 2.1 to 2.61. Garden work other than the plants themselves. Arranged in the order of work:

- **Earth and soil work:** 2.1 trenching, 2.2 good earth, 2.3 sludge, 2.4 dump manure (screened 20, 16, 4.75 mm: 2.4.1 to 2.4.3), 2.5 rough and 2.7 fine dressing, 2.6 weeding, 2.8 spreading and 2.9 mixing of sludge or manure, 2.12 uprooting weeds and rank vegetation, 2.13 preparation of hedge and shrubbery beds, 2.14 digging and refilling holes (2.14.1 to 2.14.4 by hole size, 1.2 m, 90 cm, 60 cm, 45 cm), 2.20 filling trenches, 2.21 to 2.23 excavation in malba, bajri path and WBM road, 2.24 flooding, 2.25 cattle manure, 2.28 mounds.
- **Grass and lawn:** 2.10 grassing with Doob grass (2.10.1 in rows, 2.10.2 turf), 2.11 renovating lawns, 2.33 Neelgiri / Mexican grass turf, 2.34 Doob grass turf, 2.35 supply of grass turf only, 2.48 weeding of lawn.
- **Tree guards, edging, pots:** 2.15 brick tree guard, 2.16, 2.26 and 2.27 M.S. tree guards, 2.17 and 2.18 guards from coal tar drums, 2.19 brick edging, 2.29 and 2.30 cement concrete pots, 2.31 filling empty pots (by pot size), 2.32 filling polythene bags (by bag size).
- **Maintenance:** 2.36 and 2.37 hedge and edge cutting, 2.38 topiary, 2.39 and 2.40 complete garden maintenance, 2.41 kitchen garden, 2.42 trees, 2.43 shrubs, 2.44 to 2.47 potted plants (nursery, ground floor, cement pots, multi storey buildings), 2.59 cleaning and sweeping, 2.61 pruning of trees and shrubs.
- **Other:** 2.49 floral foam, 2.50 river pebbles, 2.51 and 2.52 cut flower arrangement and stage decoration, 2.53 water tanker, 2.54 to 2.56 anti termite treatment of trees and lawn, 2.57 plantation of plants at site, 2.58 Tuflex garden fencing, 2.60 removal of garden waste.

How to pick: a planting job needs the plant from chapters 3 to 11, plus 2.14 for the hole, 2.57 for the plantation work and 2.9 or 2.8 for soil mixture. For a lawn use 2.10, 2.33 or 2.34 on a prepared bed (2.5, 2.7). Use the maintenance items for a defined period, quantity in months.

## Category: FOLIAGE AND SHADE LOVING PLANTS

Chapter 3, items 3.1 to 3.116, unit No. Indoor and shade foliage plants (Aglaonema, Philodendron, Zanzibar gem and similar) in pots, listed by botanical name. Item text gives the plant, the height, the number of leaves or plants in a pot and the pot size. Pick by plant name and height. Do not add a pot item: the pot is part of the item.

## Category: SEASONAL PLANTS

Chapter 4, items 4.1 to 4.146, unit No. Seasonal flowering plants "in full bloom" in earthen or plastic pots (Allyssum, Anemone, Antirrhinum, Aster, Chrysanthemum, Cineraria, Cosmos, Gaillardia, Zinnia and others), in alphabetical order. Several varieties of one flower have separate items by size or variety. For seedlings in trays for planting in beds, use chapter 10.

## Category: ORNAMENTAL PLANTS

Chapter 5, items 5.1 to 5.71, unit No. Ornamental foliage plants, shrubs and palms displayed in pots (Acalypha, palms such as Mascarena and Phoenix roebelenii, and others), alphabetical. Rates run from about 40 to 6786 per plant: large palms and specimen plants are the dear items. Check the height in the item text.

## Category: GROUND COVERS PLANTS

Chapter 6, items 6.1 to 6.37, unit No. Ground cover plants (Alpinia, Alternanthera, Asparagus, Ophiopogon, Wadelia and others) "providing and stacking" in 15 to 25 cm pots or bags. For grass use chapter 2, not this chapter.

## Category: TREE PLANTS

Chapter 7, items 7.1 to 7.96, unit No. Forest and avenue trees (Acacia, Albizia, Alstonia, Kigelia, Lagerstroemia and others), typically 150 to 165 cm high in 25 cm bags, alphabetical. Plantation, hole digging and tree guards come from chapter 2.

## Category: SHRUBS

Chapter 8, items 8.1 to 8.80, unit No. Flowering and hedge shrubs (Bauhinia, Beloperone, Caesalpinia, Jatropha and others), typically 30 to 75 cm high in 20 cm pots or poly bags, alphabetical. Several items are the same shrub in a different height or container.

## Category: CREEPER PLANTS

Chapter 9, items 9.1 to 9.18, unit No. Climbers and creepers (Allamanda, Bignonia, Bougainvillea, Passiflora, Petrea and others) 30 to 45 cm high in 20 cm pots or bags.

## Category: ANNUAL FLOWER SEEDLINGS/CUTTINGS

Chapter 10, unit tray (each tray has 100 plants). Two headings: 10.1 winter seasonal flower seedlings (10.1.1 to 10.1.32, Alyssum, Antirrhinum, Aster, Nasturtium, Nemesia and others) and 10.2 summer and rainy season seedlings (10.2.1 to 10.2.10). Use the child item for the flower. For flowering plants already in pots use chapter 4.

## Category: MEDICINAL PLANTS

Chapter 11, items 11.1 to 11.33, unit No. Medicinal and herb plants (Anjeer, Curry leaf, Ashwagandha, Kalmegh, Aparajita and others), in 20 to 25 cm poly bags, with some larger plants (Justicia, Ficus panda) at the end.

## Category: TRANSPLANTATION OF TREES

Chapter 12, one heading 12.1 (transplantation within 2 km, including excavation) with four priced children by girth of the tree: 12.1.1 up to 50 cm, 12.1.2 beyond 50 cm and up to 90 cm, 12.1.3 beyond 90 cm and up to 150 cm, 12.1.4 beyond 150 cm. Unit tree. Rates rise from about 4419 to 25785.

## Category: CIVIL ITEMS

Codes C2.1 to C13.61.1, 34 rows (22 priced). The civil items the horticulture book repeats from DSR 2023, with codes prefixed "C" (C2.1.1 is DSR 2023 2.1.1). Contents: earth work excavation (C2.1, C2.6 to C2.8), sand filling in plinth (C2.27), surface dressing and ploughing (C2.28, C2.29), clearing jungle and grass (C2.31 to C2.33, C2.33.1 to C2.33.4 tree cutting by girth), RCC in plinth (C5.1.3), centering (C5.9.15), steel reinforcement (C5.22.1), bamboo jaffery fencing (C9.110), structural steel (C10.2), punning (C13.18), priming (C13.50.3), coal tar (C13.59) and synthetic enamel paint (C13.61.1). For any other civil work use DSR 2023, not this category.

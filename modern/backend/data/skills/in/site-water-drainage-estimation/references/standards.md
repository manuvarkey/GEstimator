# Site water and drainage estimation — standards register

Use the adopted CPHEEO Manual on Water Supply and Treatment Systems, Manual on Sewerage and Sewage Treatment Systems, or Manual on Storm Water Drainage for the corresponding network. Record the authority, adopted edition and project clause for demand, rainfall, return period, roughness, velocity, pressure and cover. These criteria are project inputs here. The arithmetic examples use supplied dimensions and assumptions. Check the municipal connection conditions, construction specification and SOR independently.

Checked for the storm example: CPHEEO Manual on Storm Water Drainage Systems (2019), Part A, 4.4.1.1–4.4.1.3 and equation 4.1. Its peak-flow formula gives m³/h for hectares and mm/h; divide by 3.6 for L/s. Use intensity from the local intensity-duration-frequency curve at the time of concentration and chosen return period. Sections 5.2 and 5.3.6 cover conduit flow and partly full circular pipes. For water and sewerage the governing local numerical limits still require project evidence.

For foul sewers, the installed selection card uses the ultimate part-full criterion in CPHEEO Part A §3.15.5 and calculates capacities from circular geometry. For pressure pipe, record the actual product grade, SDR, PN and bore. The pressure-card dimensions and friction factor are illustrative project inputs.

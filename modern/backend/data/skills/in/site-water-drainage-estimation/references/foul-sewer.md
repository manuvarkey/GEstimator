# Select a foul sewer at part flow

For each reach sum upstream sanitary flows once, apply the adopted sanitary peak basis and add infiltration separately. Record initial and ultimate flows, upstream/downstream invert levels, surveyed run, material, roughness, internal bore and authority minimum diameter. For uniform gravity flow use invert fall/run. Backwater, pumping or a downstream restriction requires a different hydraulic check.

For a circular bore D metres and fill fraction y = depth/D, θ = 2 acos(1 − 2y) in radians, wetted area A = D²(θ − sin θ)/8, wetted perimeter P = Dθ/2. Manning velocity v = (A/P)^(2/3) × √S / n; capacity = 1000Av in L/s. Use positive hydraulic slope S for uniform free-surface flow. For operating velocity, solve for y at the actual flow on the increasing-flow branch up to the allowed fill.

Under CPHEEO Manual on Sewerage and Sewage Treatment Systems (2013), Part A §3.15.5, use at most 0.8D at ultimate peak for the circular sewer. At n=0.013, slope 0.005 and that fill, calculated capacities for **150, 200 and 250 mm internal bores** are **10.53, 22.67 and 41.10 L/s**. Scale capacity by (0.013/n) × √(S/0.005) for the same bore and fill.

Example S1: ultimate 14 L/s. The 150 mm candidate exceeds allowed depth. The 200 mm candidate flows at about 0.56D and 0.77 m/s. Carry the 200 mm bore as provisional if the adopted minimum size permits it. Check self-cleansing at the relevant initial flow and permissible maximum velocity; the capacity at 0.8D does not establish either check. Confirm the supplier's internal diameter, not only its DN. A larger mandated public sewer minimum overrides this shortlist. Return the reach length, grade, pipe class, invert levels, chambers by unique ID and outstanding checks.

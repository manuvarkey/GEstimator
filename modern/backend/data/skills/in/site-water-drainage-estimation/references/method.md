# Site water and drainage estimation — method

Measure pipe by the item's length convention at nodes. Use authorised trench sections, excavation to underside of bedding, bedding volume, pipe displacement and separately specified surround to derive backfill. Record rock, shoring, dewatering, disposal, tests and reinstatement where separately payable. Count chambers by node ID and specify depths and covers.

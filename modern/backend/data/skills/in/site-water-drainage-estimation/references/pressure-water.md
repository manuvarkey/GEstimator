# Size a pressure-water reach

Establish peak duty at the critical outlet, available inlet pressure head **at that duty**, elevation rise, length, fittings and required residual head. For a population estimate, peak L/s = population × agreed litres/person/day × peak factor / 86,400. Separate simultaneous fire duty where required. Loop-fed networks need flows and pressures from the network solution.

Use the project's approved material, dimension series and working pressure. For PE water pipe specify grade, SDR, nominal outside diameter and PN under the adopted IS 4984 edition. Obtain wall thickness and tolerances for the proposed product. Bore = outside diameter − twice wall thickness. Use the minimum available bore if the tolerance matters. A nominal diameter used as hydraulic bore can understate head loss.

At each candidate bore D (m), v = 4Q/(πD²), Q in m³/s. Residual head = inlet pressure head − elevation rise − (fL/D + ΣK)v²/(2g) for equal inlet/outlet velocity heads. Include velocity-head change where sections differ. Use g=9.81 m/s² and the **Darcy** friction factor for the pipe and Reynolds number. Check velocity window, residual head, static and surge pressure, temperature derating and utility minimum size.

Example W1: compare two candidates offered by an illustrative project schedule. Peak 1.50 L/s, length 80 m, inlet head 16 m, rise 2 m, required residual 10 m. Preliminary f=0.025, ΣK=3, specified velocity window 0.6–2.0 m/s. These are project assumptions.

| OD × assumed wall, mm | Bore mm | Velocity m/s | Residual m |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 40 × 3.7 | 32.6 | 1.80 | 3.41 |
| 50 × 4.6 | 40.8 | 1.15 | 10.51 |

Carry 80 m of the 50 mm OD candidate to the reach schedule. Its small head margin warrants checking guaranteed bore and fitting losses. Missing inlet pressure leaves the residual check open. Match the schedule by OD, material and pressure class; record fittings and testing against actual item inclusions.

# External electrical estimation — method

Split the route at changes of installation, occupancy, crossing and owner. Record trench, number of ducts, occupied ways, cables per way, rises, authorised slack and termination type. A spare duct adds duct length only. For each laying item check whether excavation, covering, warning tape, backfill, ducts or pulling are already included. Assign trench and reinstatement once even where civil and electrical schedules overlap.

Count pillars, poles, brackets, heads, foundations, control gear and earth connections by asset ID. Reconcile poles to heads and circuits, including multi-arm poles. For road lighting, obtain road class, adopted luminance or illuminance, uniformity and glare criteria, mounting geometry, maintenance and outdoor luminaire photometry. Measure a supplied layout; leave pole spacing provisional until the outdoor calculation is checked. Derive earthing quantities from electrode, bonding and test-link details.

For a utility connection record voltage, point of supply, metering, ownership, sanctioned capacity, utility estimate and any applicant-supplied works. Keep utility charges separate from measured installation items.

For a shared trench record route ID, measured segment, quantity owner and inclusion decision beside each BOQ line. If a composite laying item includes excavation and backfill, keep the physical trench dimensions as supporting records and allocate zero additional payable excavation to civil. Record each spare duct separately from its occupied cable ways.

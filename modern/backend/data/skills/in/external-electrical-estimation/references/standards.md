# External electrical estimation — standards register

CPWD General Specifications for Electrical Works Part II External 2023 identifies cable, trenchless, road-lighting and pole work. Confirm its adopted work-specific clause and the attached item's inclusions before pricing. IS 3043:2026, 4.5 and 13.1 support bonding and soil data checks. Apply the project's distributor rules and the applicable CEA safety regulations and amendments. Outdoor photometric and utility design limits require the authority's adopted criteria. No local distributor, soil resistivity or outdoor road class is assumed.

CPWD External 2023, Chapter 2, laying in pipes and closed ducts (immediately before 2.6.10): for each cable run the drawing/laying quantity uses occupied pipe or duct length. Record purchased cable and extra exposed cable separately. Chapter 6, 6.14.2 covers field measurement of road lighting. [CPWD Part II External 2023](https://cpwd.gov.in/Publication/Part_II_External_2023.pdf)

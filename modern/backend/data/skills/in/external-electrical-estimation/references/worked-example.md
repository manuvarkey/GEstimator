# External electrical estimation — worked example

Route P1–P2, plan E4 rev B. One 50 m trench carries two continuous ducts. Only the first duct contains a four-core feeder. Book 50 trench metres, 100 duct metres and 50 cable metres on the horizontal route. If two 1.5 m end rises are separately documented in cable length, the cable material total becomes 53 m. Under the CPWD closed-duct laying rule the pulling item still measures 50 m for the single occupied way. Check the item covering the rises separately. Count two end terminations when the laying item excludes them.

The existing road crossing occupies 5 m of this route, not another 5 m. Split trench records into 45 m verge and 5 m crossing. At 0.8 m width the electrical package owns 4 m² road reinstatement. Mark this crossing ID on the road schedule. With two poles and two heads per pole, record two poles and four luminaires; check pole-item inclusions before adding brackets or foundations.

If the selected SOR has a bare duct item but the crossing requires a tested multi-way duct bank with concrete encasement, record the required assembly and measured length as unmatched until the parent and child descriptions or a separately requested analysis cover it. Keep the supported verge item and crossing ownership visible in the draft.

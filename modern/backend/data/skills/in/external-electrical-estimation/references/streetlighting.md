# Streetlighting radial

Record road class and width, conflict areas, adopted maintained lighting criteria, pole setback, height, overhang, tilt, optics and maintenance factor. Test candidate pole positions with the actual luminaire photometry for the adopted luminance/illuminance, uniformity and glare criteria. Until checked, identify pole count and spacing as a layout assumption. CPWD External 2023 Chapter 6 includes measurement of lighting performance.

Example single-phase radial, 230 V, from pillar to P1, P2 and P3 at chainages 25, 60 and 90 m, one, two and one identical heads. Each draws a supplied 0.50 A at duty. From the pillar, segment currents are 2.0, 1.5 and 0.5 A. Supplier's single-phase loop drop coefficient is 11 mV/A/m at this duty. Drop to P3 = .011 × (25 × 2 + 35 × 1.5 + 30 × .5) = 1.29 V, or 0.56%. Add actual source drop. Check inrush and protective disconnection at the last pole. For multiple independent radials allocate complete radials across phases and check each pillar phase and neutral. For a shared-neutral multi-phase backbone solve phase and neutral drops for the actual asymmetric currents and phase angles.

Book 90 m horizontal three-core L/N/PE cable before measured rises, three poles and foundations, two single brackets, one double bracket and four luminaires subject to item inclusions. Assign ducts, chambers, controller/contactor, surge protection, local fusing, access doors, PE connections and reinstatement by route and pole ID. Moving P2 after photometric review changes both adjacent spans and their voltage drops.

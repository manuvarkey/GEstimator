# Plaster, pointing and finishes

Governing part: **IS 1200 (Part 12):1976 Plastering and pointing, third revision** (reaffirmed). Clauses checked against that text. For whitewashing and distempering check Part 13:1994, 3.2, including the face treatment and opening size. For painting covered by Part 15:1987, 3.1.1, openings up to 0.5 sqm each stay in the area and their edges are included. Measure them to the project specification and the adopted item.

## Inputs

- Room finish schedule: plaster type, thickness and mix for each surface, ceiling finish, paint system.
- Faces to be finished: inside, outside, one face or both.
- Room plans and sections: lengths between walls, heights from floor or skirting to ceiling.
- Door and window schedule: opening sizes, frame width against wall thickness, reveal widths on each face.
- Heights: work over 10 m above ground or datum.

## Measurement (Part 12)

- Unit: **sqm** unless otherwise described (3.6). Dimensions to 0.01 m, areas to 0.01 sqm (2.4).
- Measure roofs, ceilings and walls separately (3.2). Classify by material, mix, coats and thickness (3.1).
- **Walls:** length between walls or partitions, height from the top of the floor or skirting to the ceiling. Take dimensions before plastering. Deduct the depth of cornices or coves (3.12). Add the sides of pilasters and projections (3.12.1).
- **Ceilings:** between walls or partitions, dimensions before plastering. Deduct the width covered by cornices or coves (3.9). Stair soffits count as ceilings (3.10).
- Bands, cornices and narrow work not part of general plastering: running metres up to 30 cm in width or girth, sqm above (3.4).
- Plaster more than 10 m above ground or datum is measured separately in 5 m stages. Interior plaster of buildings is measured separately for each storey (3.5).

## Openings and ends (3.8)

| Opening or end, each | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ends of joists, beams, posts, girders, steps up to 0.5 sqm | No deduction. No addition for finishing around them. |
| Opening up to 0.5 sqm | No deduction. No addition for jambs, soffits or sills. |
| Opening over 0.5 sqm and up to 3 sqm | Deduct as below. No addition for jambs, soffits or sills. |
| Opening over 3 sqm | Deduct on each face. **Measure** the jambs, soffits and sills (3.8.2). |

Deductions for openings over 0.5 sqm and up to 3 sqm (3.8.1(b)):

1. Both faces plastered with the same plaster: deduct from **one face only**.
2. Faces with different plaster, or plaster on one face and pointing on the other: deduct from the side with the narrower reveal only. Where the reveals are equal, deduct 50 % of the opening from each face.
3. One face plastered, the other not: deduct in full if the reveal on the plastered side is narrower. Make no deduction if the reveals are equal or the plastered reveal is wider.
4. Door frame as wide as the wall, or projecting beyond it: deduct in full from each plastered face.

Exclude the extra width of rebated reveals from the opening area (Note to 3.8.2). Pointing follows the same rules (4.6).

These are IS 1200 rules. Check the project specification and the item: some departments specify their own deduction rules for plaster or for painting.

## Selecting DSR 2023 items

Read `describe_source section=category:FINISHING`. In summary: 12 mm cement plaster 13.1 (internal walls), 15 mm 13.2 or 20 mm 13.3 (rough or external faces), 6 mm 13.16 (ceilings and concrete surfaces); extras 13.21 (waterproofing compound) and 13.22 (height over 10 m). Paint items are separate for new surfaces (13.44 to 13.86) and old surfaces (13.87 onward). Pick the primer item where the specification calls for one.

# Building assemblies

Carry the room, opening, roof zone or bar mark and drawing revision onto every record. Split each assembly by payable item. A composite system may already include screed, primer, membrane laps, fittings or protection.

- Floor F1, 6 × 4 m, example detail A4 rev B. A separately payable 50 mm uniform screed is 1.20 m³ if the item pays by volume. A specified-thickness finish paid by area is 24 m². Take skirting along the finished perimeter less door breaks. For sloping roofs use the surface basis in the item and measured falls.
- Roof R1 with a 6 × 4 m deck and a continuous 0.30 m vertical return on all four sides gives 24 m² horizontal and 6 m² vertical before openings. Keep those records separate. Book laps, fillets, outlets and protective layers according to the system description. An unspecified upstand height leaves the return quantity open.
- Record frame, shutter, glazing and hardware by opening ID, material and size. Read the complete item before entering accessories. For a 0.50 × 0.60 m opening through 230 mm brickwork the 0.30 m² opening deducts 0.069 m³ before rounding under IS 1200 Part 3:1976, 4.1.4. Under Part 12:1976, 3.8.1, this opening leaves the wall plaster gross and adds no reveals.
- BBS rev C, mark B12: 10 bars × 4.20 m cutting length = 42 m. With the schedule's unit mass of 0.888 kg/m the quantity is 37.296 kg before rounding. Record diameter, grade and member. Identify bends and authorised laps already in the cutting length. Separate authorised chairs by mark and exclude procurement waste from measured steel. See Part 8:1993, 11.1–11.3.

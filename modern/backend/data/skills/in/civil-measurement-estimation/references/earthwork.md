# Earthwork

Governing part: **IS 1200 (Part 1):1992 Earthwork, fourth revision** (reaffirmed). Clauses 4.1–4.2 address authorised excavation dimensions and the use of levels. Check the actual schedule item for its included lead, lift and unit.

## Inputs

- Foundation plan and sections: footing sizes, bottom levels, existing ground levels.
- Soil investigation or site classification: soil, ordinary rock, hard rock (blasting allowed or prohibited).
- Authorised working space, side slopes and shoring, as specified.
- Disposal location and lead, filling material source.

## Measurement

- Excavation is measured in **cum** to the authorised dimensions. For work listed in 4.2.5, measure the lesser of actual and authorised excavation, including the applicable working space under 4.2.5.1. Identify specified battering and benching separately under 4.2.6.
- Take ground levels before work begins where the ground is not level. Use the levels for the volume.
- Lift is measured from ground level. Excavation up to 1.5 m deep is included in the item. Extra lift is measured in units of 1.5 m or part of it for soil excavation and deposit at ground level (Part 1, 5.2). Check the exception for lift inherent in the lead.
- Lead is measured by the shortest practical route (Part 1, 5.1).
- Hard rock may be measured in stacks with a pre-agreed deduction for voids, where the specification allows.
- Filling is measured in cum as consolidated, with a deduction for voids where specified.

## Selecting DSR 2023 items

Read `describe_source section=category:EARTH WORK`. In summary:

- **Area or trench:** excavation over areas (more than 1.5 m wide and 10 sqm on plan) is 2.6 or 2.7. Excavation in foundation trenches and drains (up to 1.5 m wide or 10 sqm on plan) is 2.8 or 2.9. A lift up to 1.5 m and disposal within 50 m are included. Add 2.26 for each further 1.5 m lift.
- Pipe and cable trenches per metre, depth up to 1.5 m: 2.10 (soil), 2.13 (rock), by pipe diameter.
- Filling with available excavated earth 2.25; with brought earth 2.25A; sand under floors 2.27.
- Anti-termite treatment 2.34 and 2.35.
- Work in or under water or in foul positions: the percentage items 2.24.1 and 2.24.2 apply to the base item's rate, not as a rate × quantity.

## Checks

- Classify the volume by soil or rock and by area or trench before matching.
- Do not add carriage for disposal within the lead the item includes.
- Back-filling volume = excavation - volume of the work below ground level (footings, PCC, plinth masonry).

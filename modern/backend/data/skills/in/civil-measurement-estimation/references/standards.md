# Standards register (civil)

The guidance cites only the clauses listed as checked. Before you cite a clause in a bill or report, confirm the edition the contract adopts and any amendments issued since.

## Cited from the bundled database notes

DSR 2023 item codes, units and inclusions come from the DSR 2023 database notes in GEstimator. The notes were checked against the printed DSR 2023. Read them with `describe_source`. DSR 2023 follows the CPWD Specifications 2019 Vol. I and II; Volume I, 5.3.4 and Table 5.4 are the checked reinforcement measurement provisions. Identify authorised bar lengths by diameter and use the stipulated unit-mass basis, including the provision for actual weight within tolerance. Exclude unauthorised laps and cutting waste. [CPWD Specifications 2019, Volume I](https://pwd.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/cpwd-specs2019v1.pdf)

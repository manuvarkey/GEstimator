# Standards register (electrical)

Before you cite a clause in a bill or report, confirm the edition the contract adopts and any correction slips issued since.

## Checked against the published text

| Document | Edition checked | Clauses used |
|---|---|---|
| CPWD General Specifications for Electrical Works Part I Internal | 2023 (released 12.07.2023) | 3.3.1, 3.3.2, 3.3.3 (circuit, submain and other wiring), 3.4.1 to 3.4.8 (point wiring definition, scope, measurement, groups, sockets, group, twin and bell control), 3.9.3 (socket ratings), 3.10.1 to 3.10.3 (circuit capacity) |
| Energy Conservation and Sustainable Building Code (ECSBC, BEE) | 2024 | Scope 2 (100 kW / 120 kVA, commercial and office), 7.2.2 (interior lighting controls), 7.2.4 (centralised control), 7.3.2, 7.3.3, Tables 7.1 to 7.6 (interior lighting power) |
| SP 7 National Building Construction Standards (NBCS) | 2026, fourth revision | Part D Section 2, 5.4.4.3 (sub-circuit limits) |

## Cited from the bundled database notes

DSR (E&M) 2025 item codes, units and inclusions come from the DSR (E&M) 2025 database notes in GEstimator. The notes were checked against the printed book (Correction Slip No. 1 of 27-11-2025 applied). Read them with `describe_source`.

## Reviewed, not cited

SP 30:2023 (National Electrical Code), IS 732:2019 (wiring installations) and IS 3043:2026 (earthing) were available but are design and installation codes; the measurement rules here come from the CPWD specification. Apply the relevant design checks alongside the adopted payment specification, including on CPWD projects. See [LV design sources](gestimator://skills/in/preliminary-lv-distribution-design/references/standards.md).

## Energy-code basis

The CPWD specification's lighting power tables (2.10.2, Tables 2.1 to 2.6) quote ECBC 2018. Identify the applicable energy code, state adoption, occupancy and contract edition before choosing a table.

## Still to verify

List these as review items in the draft. Do not look them up online during an estimate.

- Additional external and plant clauses beyond the [checked external work provisions](gestimator://skills/in/external-electrical-estimation/references/standards.md).
- ENS 2024 (the residential code the DSR (E&M) 2025 also cites), for residential projects that ECSBC does not cover.
- ECSBC 2024 sets no interior luminaire efficacy; the LED efficacy band comes from the project specification.

# Raised roof equipment and service entries

Start with the class and conductor IDs from the [roof layout](gestimator://skills/in/preliminary-lightning-protection-design/references/layout.md). Plot elevated tank corners, handrails, PV modules, metal supports and inverter/DC routes in plan and elevation. Check rolling-sphere interception and required separation along each path for the adopted Part 3 edition. Identify which original runs move, which remain and where an isolated mast or bonded solution changes the design. Check mast wind loading, fixing and roof waterproofing.

Example revised detail for the same 30 × 20 m roof: the approved elevation shows two isolated masts protecting the tank and PV edge. It retains the original 150 m mesh, adds two 4 m conductor connections, and leaves eight downs and the 108 m common ring intact. Book two mast assemblies and 8 m additional measured conductor. Verify insulating supports and separation along the new paths, and whether mast kits include connections. These quantities depend on the supplied elevation and mast arrangement.

Trace utility power and telecom service entries to the equipotential bonding location. Identify SPD duty and compatibility per interface. For PV check DC polarity, maximum cold open-circuit voltage, short-circuit rating, separation and current-sharing design; also check the inverter AC side. List entry power SPD, signal protector, PV DC and AC devices separately with status and package owner. Credit any SPD already in the board or inverter only after verifying its declared protection and location. Book shared ring segments once; identify additional bonds and test points by endpoint.

# Preliminary LV distribution design — worked example

Example F1, drawing E2 rev A. Balanced 400 V three-phase feeder, 18 kW electrical input, PF 0.90, supplied demand factor 0.80. Demand = 14.4 kW. Current = 14,400 / (√3 × 400 × 0.90) = 23.1 A.

Trial cable: four-core 10 mm² PVC-insulated copper, three loaded conductors, one circuit in surface conduit (method B2). Assume negligible neutral harmonic load. IS 732:2019 Table 23 gives 46 A at its reference air temperature. At the example ambient of 40 °C, Table 33 gives a PVC factor of 0.87. With no grouping or other corrections the capacity is 46 × 0.87 = 40.02 A. A proposed 32 A device with a supplied operating current of 46.4 A meets both overload checks: 23.1 < 32 < 40.02 and 46.4 < 1.45 × 40.02. Record the separate protective conductor route or other verified protective path.

The example supplier gives a three-phase drop coefficient of 3.8 mV/A/m for the cable at the stated duty. A 50 m run drops 4.39 V or 1.10% of 400 V. Add the supplied upstream 0.70% and compare 1.80% with the example project budget of 3%. The overload and drop checks support a provisional selection. Fault level, clearing time and earth-loop data are missing. Hold breaking capacity, withstand and disconnection open. Book one 50 m multicore cable run and identify its two ends.

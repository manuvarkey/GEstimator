# Preliminary LV distribution design — standards register

BIS IS 732:2019, 4.4.4.1 covers overload coordination, 4.4.5 covers short-circuit protection, Annex S covers current capacities and Annex Y covers voltage drop. The example uses Table 20 method B2, Table 23 (printed page 180) and Table 33 (printed page 190). Match the installation and conductor conditions before using a table row. BIS SP 30:2023 Part 1 Section 12 relates the overcurrent checks to IS 732. BIS IS 3043:2026, 4.5 and 13.1 cover bonding and soil resistivity measurements. Do not infer an electrode count from connected kW.

Identify applicable requirements of the CEA Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply Regulations 2023, amendments including 2026, and the local distributor. Applicability depends on the installation and supply point. Verify settings and disconnection against project-specific fault data.

The feeder selection card covers a subset of Tables 21–24 (copper, two or three loaded conductors), Table 33 (ambient air), Table 36 item 1 (uniform grouping), and Annex Y including Table 47. Service-temperature resistance and reactance are screening inputs. Manufacturer data and actual installation conditions determine their applicability.

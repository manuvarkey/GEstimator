# Internal plumbing and sanitation

Use the adopted SP 7:2026 Part E water supply and drainage provisions and local water/sewer conditions. List fixtures by room/floor, including flush type, hot/cold service and required outlet pressure. Assign fixture units under the adopted method, convert cumulative units to probable flow for each branch/riser, and add continuous process demand separately. Obtain actual simultaneous flow or the adopted conversion curve. A count of taps alone cannot determine peak flow.

Start the pressure balance at the lowest available operating head. Deduct static rise, meter, backflow device, valves, pipe and fitting losses; compare remaining head at the critical outlet with fixture requirements. Use actual internal bore. For example, approved peak 0.60 L/s through 25 mm bore gives velocity 1.22 m/s. With supplied Darcy factor .025, 15 m straight length and total fitting K = 6, losses are about 1.14 + 0.46 m. With 14 m available head, 4 m rise and 2 m meter loss the outlet retains 6.40 m against an example 5 m requirement. Check the adopted velocity limit, water hammer and other critical paths. See [pressure-water method](gestimator://skills/in/site-water-drainage-estimation/references/pressure-water.md).

On the sanitary side assign fixture discharge units by appliance, trap and connection type. Select branch and stack diameters from the adopted capacity/gradient and venting arrangement. Check WC branch minimum, trap seals, offsets, cleaning access and invert at the receiving chamber. Record missing fixture-unit, capacity or minimum-bore data as open sizing inputs. Keep roof and foul drainage distinct.

Two-storey toilet block, each floor with two cistern WCs and two basins. For a supplied layout with one isolating valve per floor, two WC connections and two cold basin connections per floor, count four WCs, four basins, eight cold connections and two floor valves. If the measured cold branches are 8 m per floor and the shared riser 4 m, book 16 m branch and 4 m riser split by actual bore. Record sanitary branches separately from the measured shared soil stack, vent extension, cleanouts and sleeves. Take off one trap per basin only where excluded from the fixture item. Assign the building drain to chamber inlet C1 to plumbing and the chamber and outgoing site reach to [site drainage](gestimator://skills/in/site-water-drainage-estimation). Include pressure/leak tests for each system and reinstatement by location.

For common cistern WCs and basins use the [offline fixture-unit example](gestimator://skills/in/internal-plumbing-estimation/references/toilet-block.md) before selecting a bore.

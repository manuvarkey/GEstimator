# Fire and life-safety estimate

Record jurisdiction, adopted fire provisions, occupancy and mixed uses, measured height, floor and basement areas, access, compartmentation, existing protection and approved fire strategy. Use the applicable occupancy schedule in adopted building/fire rules and SP 7:2026 Part F where adopted. For each candidate system record applicability, trigger or approval reference, drawing revision, coverage zones, estimate owner and missing inputs. An unknown trigger warrants a separate provisional option.

For hydrants, sprinklers and other suppression systems obtain design demand, simultaneous duties, duration, residual pressure and water supply. Size usable storage from the governing water balance. Select pump duty from static rise, pipe/valve losses and residual head at the critical point. Determine duty, standby and jockey pumps from the approved arrangement. For alarm, derive device/loop quantities from the actual coverage plan, isolators, addressing and interfaces. For emergency lighting identify escape routes, emergency supply, duration and photometric checks. Measure tested penetration seals by wall/floor rating and service geometry.

Example of a supplied strategy: concurrent demands 15 and 10 L/s for 60 minutes with no credited inflow give 90 m³ usable storage. Deduct unusable volume from an existing tank before crediting capacity. This example assumes both services operate for the stated duration; take the actual approved duty profile for the project. A shared tank shell belongs once to civil, fire pipework and pump set to mechanical, the dedicated pump feeder and changeover to electrical. Allocate pump controls and testing explicitly. Trace the released alarm interface to the receiving panel and record which package includes its wiring.

Return a system-by-zone BOQ with approved, assumed or pending quantities, plus integrated commissioning and authority inspection scope. Use [supply planning](gestimator://skills/in/preliminary-supply-planning) for essential power and [electrical takeoff](gestimator://skills/in/electrical-measurement-estimation) for cable and board measurements.

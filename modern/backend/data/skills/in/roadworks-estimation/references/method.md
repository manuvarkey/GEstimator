# Roadworks estimation — method

Work from signed cut and fill areas at each surveyed section. Keep cut and fill separate. For a segment using average end areas, volume = length × (start area + end area) / 2. Insert a section at a transition through zero or at a material change. Keep unsuitable material, reuse, disposal, borrow, lead and lift traceable.

For uniform layer thickness and a linear taper use length × mean width × compacted thickness. For varying width and thickness use cross sections and the specified integration method. Record kerb returns and excluded entrances by side. Keep surfaced areas, compacted volume and delivered mass separate. Convert only with a project density and an item requiring that unit. Assign every service crossing and reinstatement to one package.

If thickness must be designed, establish road class, design traffic and life, subgrade strength, drainage and material properties. For an adopted design catalogue use its applicable traffic/subgrade band, plate and assumptions. Where a mechanistic check is required, record the trial layers, analysis results and governing response limits. Without those sources retain the known formation geometry and identify the unsupported layer dimensions.

For IRC:37-2018, 4.6.1, compute cumulative standard axles from opening-year commercial vehicles per day A, annual growth fraction r, years n, lane factor D and vehicle damage factor F. Multiply 365 × A × D × F by ((1+r)^n − 1)/r (use n for constant traffic). Divide by one million for msa. Match the directional basis of A to D. Clause 2.1 routes designs below 2 msa to IRC:SP:72 and existing-pavement overlays to separate methods. Section 12 catalogues support preliminary costing up to 50 msa. For traffic above 2 msa the detailed design uses site-specific performance checks.

# Choose an access-road section

An issued typical section takes precedence for takeoff. For a new road, identify owner, design life, heavy vehicle and fire access, cumulative equivalent standard axles (ESAL), design subgrade CBR, drainage, frost, terrain and required surface. Identify the authority's adopted method. A private campus or an urban fire route can have a mandatory paved section or vehicle loading condition independent of daily traffic.

**Low-volume gravel option.** Use this bounded shortlist only when the owner accepts the IRC:SP:72-2015 rural-road basis for that site and a gravel surface is feasible. The figures are compacted structural gravel base in mm for a non-frost site. Extracted decision points from Fig. 4, printed p. 22. Verify gravel quality, drainage and wearing course under §§6.3 and 7.1–7.3. Appendix D illustrates the 5–6 CBR, T3 case.

| Design CBR | 30,001–60,000 ESAL (T2) | 60,001–100,000 ESAL (T3) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5–6 | 250 | 275 |
| 7–9 | 175 | 225 |

Example: route A1, 80,000 design ESAL, CBR 7, compacted layer width 5 m over 100 m. The T3 structural base is 225 mm or 112.5 m³. At CBR 6 the same footprint needs 275 mm or 137.5 m³. Section 6.3 gives a typical additional 40–50 mm surface gravel allowance according to traffic and material. At 500 m² this is 20–25 m³. Price the specified wearing course separately where payable. Check rainfall, surface treatment, dust and year-round access before selecting gravel. If the T3 surface treatment or local paved access requirement changes the assembly, obtain its layer detail. Do not extrapolate this small table to frost, weaker soils or other traffic bands.

**Flexible paved option.** For an adopted IRC:37-2018 new or reconstructed flexible pavement, check §2.1 (at least 2 msa), material properties and §12 catalogue conditions. As a costing anchor only, at 10 msa and effective CBR 5%, Fig. 12.1 shows 200 mm GSB, 250 mm WMM, 80 mm bituminous base/binder and 40 mm surface. Above 2 msa the detailed design requires site-specific performance checks. A campus below this range with a mandatory paved surface needs a suitable local section or an applicable low-volume paved design. Keep formation geometry measurable while thickness is unresolved.

Return the design basis, chosen section or alternatives, layer-specific widths and chainage quantities. Keep catalogue quantities separate from the issued-design quantities for the same segment.

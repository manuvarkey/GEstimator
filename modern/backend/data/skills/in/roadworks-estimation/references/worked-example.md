# Roadworks estimation — worked example

Access road R1, section C3 rev B. Chainage 0+000 to 0+100, width 5.00 m, adopted granular layer 0.20 m compacted. Surface = 500 m² and layer = 100 m³. On the next 20 m a linear taper to 4 m gives 20 × (5 + 4) / 2 × 0.20 = 18 m³. Keep two records against the same verified volume item.

At chainage 0+040 a 0.8 m trench crosses all 5 m. For a later cut through existing surfacing the utility package owns 4 m² reinstatement; the road package books no second repair. If utilities precede new paving, measure the continuous new surface once and omit a repair allowance. Missing traffic and CBR block a new pavement design; the issued layer detail still supports takeoff.

Separate preliminary comparison: a new flexible road has supplied design traffic of 10 msa and effective subgrade CBR of 5%. For the granular-base composition, IRC:37-2018 Fig. 12.1 illustrates 200 mm granular sub-base, 250 mm WMM, 80 mm base/binder course and 40 mm surface. Across a uniform 500 m² footprint these give 100, 125, 40 and 20 m³ compacted. Check actual widths by layer before pricing. Keep the approved R1 section above separate from this catalogue comparison.

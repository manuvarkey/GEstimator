# Roadworks estimation — standards register

IRC:37-2018 covers flexible pavement design. Its applicability, issued amendments and authority adoption must be established for the road. Check the adopted IRC design plate or analysis and the contract edition of MoRTH Specifications for Road and Bridge Works or the state specification for layer measurement. Clauses 2.1, 4.6.1 and 12.1–12.3 cover applicability, cumulative design traffic and the limits of initial estimation. Figure 12.1 on printed page 36 was visually checked for the single 10 msa example. Use the adopted authority specification to price it. [IRC:37-2018](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.037.2019.pdf) Record the contract clause for compaction, conversion and payment.

One published Indian works specification provides a payment example. Its Section 401.7 measures finished granular sub-base in place by volume and includes the specified edge protection. Use that basis only where the contract adopts the provision. [RRCAT roadworks specification, Section 400](https://www.rrcat.gov.in/contact/docs/csdSpecRoadWork.pdf)

IRC:SP:72-2015, Fig. 4 (printed page 22), §6.3, §7.3 and Appendix D support the bounded gravel-road options. Confirm adoption for the project and record the surface treatment separately. [IRC low-volume rural roads guidelines](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.sp.072.2015.pdf)

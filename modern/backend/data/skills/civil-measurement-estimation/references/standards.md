# Standards register (civil)

The guidance cites only the clauses listed as checked. Before you cite a clause in a bill or report, confirm the edition the contract adopts and any amendments issued since.

## Checked against the published text

| Standard | Edition checked | Clauses used |
|---|---|---|
| IS 1200 (Part 2) Concrete works | 1974, third revision, reaffirmed 2007 | 2.4, 2.4.1, 2.6, 2.7, 4.1.1 to 4.1.8, 4.2.1, 4.2.2, 4.2.3 |
| IS 1200 (Part 3) Brickwork | 1976, third revision, reaffirmed | 2.3, 2.6, 2.7, 2.9, 3.1.1, 3.1.2, 4.1 to 4.1.6, 5.1, 7.1, 9.1, 9.2, 10.1, 12.1, 13.1, 15, 16, 19, 23 |
| IS 1200 (Part 5) Formwork | 1982, third revision, reaffirmed | 3.1, 5.1, 6.1, 6.4 |
| IS 1200 (Part 12) Plastering and pointing | 1976, third revision, reaffirmed | 2.4, 3.1, 3.2, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6, 3.8.1, 3.8.2, 3.9, 3.10, 3.12, 4.6 |

The checked copies are the public, scanned BIS texts. Their OCR has errors. One value read from the OCR, the half-brick fraction limit in Part 3, 4.1.2, still needs a check against the printed page.

## Cited from the bundled database notes

DSR 2023 item codes, units and inclusions come from the DSR 2023 database notes in GEstimator. The notes were checked against the printed DSR 2023. Read them with `describe_source`. DSR 2023 follows the CPWD Specifications 2019 Vol. I and II; their clause numbers are **not** cited here.

## Still to verify

List these as review items in the draft. Do not look them up online during an estimate.

- IS 1200 (Part 1) Earthwork, 1992: the clause numbers in the earthwork reference (the scan is two-column and poorly read).
- IS 1200 (Part 8) Steel work and iron work: reinforcement measurement, laps and chairs.
- IS 1200 (Parts 13 and 15): whitewashing, distempering and painting.
- Draft revisions: BIS has circulated draft fourth revisions of Parts 2 and 3. Confirm the edition in force.
- CPWD Specifications 2019: the measurement clauses for each work, which can differ from IS 1200 and govern DSR work.

# Distribution, cables and earthing

Item codes and inclusions come from the DSR (E&M) 2025 database notes, which were checked against the printed book. Read the category notes with `describe_source` before choosing items.

## Inputs

- Single-line diagram and DB schedules: board type, ways, incomer, outgoing MCB ratings and poles, RCCBs, isolators.
- Cable schedule: cable type, cores, size, route and laying method.
- Earthing layout: electrode type, number, earth lead route.
- Panel and switchgear ratings: current, breaking capacity, poles.

## Switchgear and boards (category MCCB, MCB & DB'S)

- A DB item is the **empty board**. Count each MCB, RCCB and isolator separately: SPN DBs 2.3, horizontal TPN DBs 2.4, vertical TPN DBs 2.5, MCBs in existing DBs 2.10, RCCBs 2.14 (DP) and 2.15 (FP), isolators 2.12 and 2.13, blanking plates 2.11.
- "Way" counts MCB positions. TPN DBs are written "4 + 12" (incomer + outgoing). Choose the board by the ways the schedule needs, including spares.
- MCCBs in existing panels go by rating, breaking capacity and poles (2.2).

## Rising mains, trays and cables

- Rising mains and bus trunking per metre (3.1, 3.5, 3.6, 3.8, 3.9, 3.13). Add end feed units and tap-off boxes separately.
- Cable trays per metre by width × depth × thickness (4.1 powder coated, 4.6 galvanised). Bends, tees and reducers are each (4.2 to 4.5, 4.7 to 4.10).
- MV cable **laying** per metre (category MV CABLE LAYING) excludes the cable. Price the cable supply separately. Choose by laying method: direct in ground with protection 10.1, in existing pipe 10.5, on wall 10.7, on tray 10.8. Excavation is included in the direct-in-ground items.
- Terminations and joints (category MV CABLE JOINTING & END TERMINATION): one cable needs **two** end terminations (11.1, 11.2). Straight-through joints are 11.3 or 11.4.

## Earthing and lightning protection

- Earth electrodes each or set: G.I. pipe 5.1 or 5.2, G.I. plate 5.3 or 5.4, copper plate 5.5 or 5.6. Choose "with charcoal or coke and salt" only where the soil needs treatment.
- Earth leads per metre (5.10 to 5.15) and loop earthing (5.16 to 5.19). Point wiring already includes loop earthing of the point; do not add it again.
- Lightning conductor items are in category LIGHTNING CONDUCTOR. Their earth electrodes come from EARTHING.

## Measuring runs

Measure each cable or wiring run along the route on the drawing. Add the vertical drops and rises into boards and equipment, and any termination allowance the specification states. Without a routing drawing, give the assumed route (for example "DB-1 at corridor, horizontal 12 m along the ceiling, 3 m drop") and mark the line provisional.
